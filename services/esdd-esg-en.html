ESDD/ESG · Permits

For international investors and lenders, we perform environmental and social due diligence (ESDD) and annual monitoring based on the Equator Principles and IFC Performance Standards — plus environmental permitting services and air, water, and noise monitoring. We connect international standards to Korean sites.

When you need this

  • Investors or lenders require ESG verification or E&S due diligence
  • International financing conditions require annual Equator Principles / IFC monitoring
  • A data center, logistics center, or power project needs E&S due diligence
  • You need a specialist to handle environmental permits for your operations

Scope of work

  • ESDD/ESG reviews based on the Equator Principles and IFC Performance Standards
  • Annual audits of E&S management systems, grievance handling, and environmental data — with lender reporting
  • Specialized assessments such as Source Vulnerability Assessments (SVA) for water resources
  • Environmental permitting services
  • Air, water, and noise measurement and monitoring

How we work

01 Standards Definition — The applicable framework (Equator Principles, IFC PS, lender requirements) is fixed and the review system designed.
02 Due Diligence & Monitoring — Site work and data verification establish performance against the standard.
03 Reporting — Reports in international formats, ready to hand to investors and lenders.
