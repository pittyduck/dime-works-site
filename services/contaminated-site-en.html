Contaminated Site Management

Where soil contamination is found — or suspected — we take the site from Phase I & II environmental site assessments through detailed soil and groundwater investigation, remediation design, and on-site management and monitoring. One team manages the site from discovery to closure, including response to remediation liability under Korea's Soil Environment Conservation Act.

When you need this

  • Contamination has been found at your site or a transaction target
  • Site history makes contamination plausible enough to require investigation
  • An administrative order requires a remediation plan and execution
  • You need independent oversight of remediation works

Scope of work

  • Phase I & II environmental site assessments
  • Detailed soil and groundwater investigation — extent and degree of contamination
  • Remediation method review, design, and cost estimation
  • On-site management, supervision, and completion verification
  • Response under the Soil Environment Conservation Act (remediation liability; statutory Soil Environment Assessment)
  • Post-remediation monitoring

How we work

01 Document Review — Site history and contamination likelihood shape the investigation design.
02 Site Survey — Sampling and analysis establish presence, extent, and degree.
03 Reporting — Remediation necessity, methods, and costs — continuing into execution management where needed.
