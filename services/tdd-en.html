Technical Due Diligence

Before you acquire or invest in an environmental facility — a landfill, an incinerator — we assess its technical value and CAPEX/OPEX feasibility through field-based due diligence. With landfill M&A active in Korea, verifying technical risk is central to the investment decision.

When you need this

  • You are evaluating the acquisition or divestiture of an environmental facility
  • You need to verify a target facility's remaining value and operating costs
  • Lenders or an investment committee require an independent technical review

Scope of work

  • Remaining landfill capacity estimation and facility life assessment
  • Liner system and leachate treatment facility condition review
  • CAPEX/OPEX feasibility for operations and post-closure management
  • Permit conditions and post-closure obligations review
  • Quantified technical risk as a basis for the investment decision

How we work

01 Document Review — Design and operating records, permit conditions, and disposal history.
02 Site Survey — Direct facility inspection and operator interviews to surface real operating risks.
03 Reporting — Quantified technical value and cost feasibility, with investment-perspective risks and alternatives.
