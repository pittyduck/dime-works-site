Environmental Due Diligence

Before you acquire, divest, or lease a plant or site in Korea, we assess the environmental risks hidden in the land and its facilities â€” from a transaction perspective. When you need this

  • You are acquiring or divesting a plant, logistics center, or commercial property
  • You are leasing an industrial site and want the environmental condition established before entry
  • A counterparty, lender, or headquarters requires an environmental due diligence report
  • The land has a past industrial use and contamination is plausible

Scope of work

  • Site and facility history; surrounding contamination sources (integrated internal/external analysis)
  • Environmental permit status and history; satellite and map review
  • Chemical and petroleum handling history; storage facilities (above/underground tanks)
  • Waste, asbestos, PCB, and other hazardous-material management review
  • Soil and groundwater contamination screening; judgment on escalation to Phase II ESA (intrusive soil & groundwater investigation)
